A university wants to develop an Employee Management
System to manage different types of employees. Create a
superclass Employee containing the following attributes and
methods:
Superclass: Employee
● name
● id
● basicSalary
● displayInfo() — displays the employee’s basic information.
● calculateSalary() — returns the employee’s basic salary.
Create one subclass:
1. AdministrativeStaff
● department
● workingHours
● calculateSalary() — calculate the staff’s salary by adding an
overtime payment if the working hours exceed the regular
working hours. Suppose: regular_hour is 40 and
overtime_rate_perHour is 500.
● displayInfo() — display all administrative staff information.
● assignDepartment() — display a message showing the
department assigned to the staff member.
Task: Create at least two AdministrativeStaff objects. Initialize
their attributes, invoke all appropriate methods, and display their
complete information and calculated salaries.
